# Whole-form evidence implementation status

## Local implementation (not deployed)

- Registered Home Survey 1.2.0 mappings without changing wording, order, ratings, required questions or professional field classifications. Earlier definitions stay intact.
- Every field/element has a suggested-answer, context-only or manual-only policy in `packages/assistant/src/forms/home-survey-evidence.ts`. The recorder displays this inventory for the selected section; it explicitly does not claim source availability.
- Historical EPC proposals cover energy bands, walls, glazing, floor descriptions, heating/hot water, certificate-reported area and Level 3 energy descriptions. Assumptions remain verbatim. Roof insulation is context-only, not a roof-covering/structural answer. Recommendations remain context-only, not automatically suitable advice.
- Canonical EPC enrichment uses bearer `/api/domestic/search`, padded UPRN equality and `/api/certificate` detail verification. Fixture-verified RdSAP 19.0, 20.0.0 and 21.0.1 construction details are decoded; both plain and language-wrapped descriptions are supported. Explicit recommendation text and GBP savings/cost structures are retained, without inventing meanings for undecoded codes. Other schemas retain basic energy bands and mark unsupported fields unavailable. Numeric construction/fuel/recommendation codes are not guessed. Requests and certificate counts are bounded; normalised cache entries exclude addresses. Legacy snapshots remain readable; legacy adapter is used explicitly by regression fixtures only.
- Customer questionnaire GET/draft/submit routes and job detail/customer portal screens. Optional answers are bounded and validated, purchase price is valuation-only. Submitted versions are immutable at the database level; corrections append. Accepted quote snapshots are untouched.
- Questionnaire JSON is read only after purpose-token/assignment authorisation and bounded to 64 KB, including streams without Content-Length. Purpose-link issuance is limited to ten per job per rolling minute under the job lock; rejected requests do not revoke the last permitted token.
- The demo launch panel includes an explicit Owner-only `prepare_evidence` operation. It rechecks current membership and the active demo/evidence flag, creates clearly fictional staff-transcribed customer submissions and skips any job with an existing draft or submission. Repeated preparation preserves corrected answers. It does not grant professional permissions, activate sources, alter pinned templates or create findings.
- Owner-only `prepare_documents` uses a selected converted quote's linked job and the normal private-document validation/storage/analysis pipeline. Its three labelled PDF fixtures cover issue-date-only, explicit works completion and blank/no-text manual review. Stable per-job fixture identifiers make repeats idempotent and preserve replaced originals. Preparation does not associate works or apply survey answers. Failed transactions clean up newly written objects, including a partial storage write; storage absence is reported rather than simulated. The demo catalogue is restricted to owners/admins and preparation controls are disabled for administrators.
- Parent customer-token validation plus separate seven-day purpose-scoped, hashed and revocable questionnaire tokens. Every operation checks the parent, organisation, linked job/property, purpose, expiry and release flag. Links expose no professional observations. Owner revocation is available through staff DELETE.
- Staff access checks current active membership and assigned job within the write transaction. Customer statements and staff-transcribed customer statements are labelled separately. Only submissions generate suggestions, never drafts.
- Suggestions reject stale field values, revised submissions and changed property identity. Accept/edit/reject retain existing professional permission checks and explicit confirmation.
- Explicit, audited upgrades of in-progress 1.0/1.1 surveys preserve answers, observations, media and report records and supersede pending suggestions. Upgrade UI requires online status and an empty device outbox/upload queue.
- Completion-document extraction distinguishes explicitly labelled works completion from certificate issue, inspection and permission dates; it requires property/works association before any year suggestion.
- Personal report name/contact and approved firm report identity settings are available. New-template proposals use only the requesting practitioner's profile, and review rechecks identity fingerprints. Background enrichment never substitutes another staff member's identity.
- Canonical postcode enrichment retains cited administrative geography with low-confidence, postcode-level caveats; it never changes property coordinates. Source activation remains an explicit operator action.
- The recorder has an assignment-filtered, read-only source-status interface showing setup required, not checked, stale, available, unavailable and no-record states with coverage and cited evidence. Field inventory responses include pending suggestion counts. Mapped questions now have individually labelled evidence disclosures with relevant customer/document/external context and source readiness. Pending suggestions appear beside their question, with no duplicate sidebar list. A shared request supplies status to the whole recorder. Offline/outbox/upload activity disables review until current inputs can be checked.

- Private customer PDF/JPEG/PNG upload and attachment downloads share purpose-token and staff-assignment gates. The multipart stream is bounded before parsing; files have signature/size validation (10 MB), checksums, per-job limits and stable upload request identifiers. Download responses use attachment, no-store, nosniff and sandbox headers. Replacements retain originals and audit metadata. Storage absence does not block questionnaire text.
- Text-bearing PDFs use the versioned local certificate-facts-v2 analyser, reading up to 30 pages without OCR or a new document-processing provider. Older v1 facts remain readable and stored analyses are not rewritten. Scans/unreadable documents remain downloadable for manual review. Context retains page/excerpt references. Explicit works dates only become completion-year proposals after an audited professional property/works association; acceptance rechecks checksum, property fingerprint and association date and rejects replaced documents.

## Pilot gate

Additive migrations 0034–0039 are local files, not applied to production. Migration 0034 creates tenant-RLS-protected draft/submission/link tables and the immutable-submission trigger; 0035 allows the labelled customer-statement proposal origin; 0036 adds personal/firm report identity and the practice-record proposal origin. Migrations 0037–0039 add tenant-RLS-protected private documents, extraction/works association metadata and an association integrity constraint.

`organisation_operational_settings.survey_evidence_enabled` defaults false. An active private demo and this flag are both required. Owners can enable it in Operations settings; management access does not grant professional recording permission. Live organisations cannot enable this release through the new interface.

Suggested stakeholder checks: enable the demo flag, submit customer information, open an assigned Home Survey, explicitly upgrade to 1.2, load evidence, inspect source labels, accept/edit/reject individually, submit a correction and verify the previous suggestion can no longer be applied. Reload customer/job pages to confirm persistence. Revoke a scoped link, disable the flag, and test unassigned and cross-tenant access.

## Remaining native work before this entire plan is complete

- Further EPC schema fixtures and verified code dictionaries for currently unavailable coded fields. Undecoded recommendation codes stay visibly unavailable; explicit text is shown as historical context, not professional advice.
- Extended provider examples; complete authenticated browser journeys, mobile/keyboard/offline regression and whole-suite release verification. Fictional questionnaire and private-document preparation are implemented; see `whole-form-stakeholder-guide.md`.
- External provider credentials, terms and data-protection approvals remain required. No national import, paid provider, production migration, purchase or deployment is authorised in this phase.
