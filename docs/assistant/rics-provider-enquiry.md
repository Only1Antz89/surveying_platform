# Prepared RICS provider enquiry

Prepared 11 October 2026. Draft only; not sent.

To: contactrics@rics.org

Subject: Surveyant — RICS Home Survey software-provider approval and authorised template provision

Dear RICS Home Survey team,

We are developing Surveyant, a platform used by surveying companies to record inspections and prepare reports. Please direct this enquiry to the team responsible for software-provider approval and permission to supply RICS Home Survey templates.

We intend to centrally supply the standard Level 1, Level 2 survey-only, Level 2 survey-and-valuation and Level 3 templates. Each company would configure the services covered by its own licences during onboarding. The booked service would select the appropriate template automatically, with manual selection for manually created jobs.

The recording interface offers the standard section layout and a guided questionnaire sharing the same answers. Our assistant proposes information from the job, property records and surveyor input. Proposed information requires surveyor review. Accepted prefills appear blue in the editor; manually entered information is black. Completed output would use the authorised report design with black answer text. Photographs and separately hosted video supplements would remain linked to the relevant job and customer.

Please confirm:

1. The provider agreement, application process, fees and approval requirements for this use.
2. Whether RICS supplies authorised production template assets directly or requires an approved supplier, and the permitted electronic, PDF and customer-portal uses.
3. The required method for validating company and individual licence entitlements, service variants and renewals.
4. Required ancillary documents, approved fonts, branding, report pagination, photographs, signatures, accessibility and continuation pages.
5. How an assistant prefill overlay and guided recording view should be assessed, including professional review and output acceptance.
6. Any requirements for video supplements, secure customer access, electronic downloads and record retention. We currently offer one-year customer access and company retention with an optional longer company archive; please identify any required changes before formal use.
7. The process for template revisions and ongoing provider approval.

We can provide recording-schema mappings, sample draft outputs, architecture details and a demonstration for review. Formal RICS-branded report issue will remain disabled until the required permissions, assets and acceptance reviews are complete.

Kind regards,
Surveyant team

## Source and next step

The recipient is the published enquiry contact in the [RICS Home Survey FAQs](https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/rics-standards-and-guidance/sector-standards/building-surveying-standards/home-surveys/home-survey-standards/home-survey-standard-frequently-asked-questions). [RICS licensing guidance](https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/home-surveys-licences) distinguishes firm licences from permitted template provision.

Sending requires the user's explicit instruction and a suitable sender account. A general instruction to complete platform work does not establish a RICS agreement. After RICS responds, obtain the specified agreement and assets, provision the private catalogue, complete field and appendix mappings, and conduct professional acceptance of the actual output. Public sample PDFs do not supply the production authorisation.
