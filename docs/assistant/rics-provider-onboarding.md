# Surveyant central RICS template provision

Surveyant does not yet have a RICS software-provider arrangement or an authorised template supplier. Companies will select their licensed RICS services in Admin during onboarding; no company template uploads are part of this workflow.

## Request to RICS or an authorised supplier

Please confirm the arrangement needed for Surveyant to centrally provide and populate standard RICS Home Survey reports for separately licensed surveying firms. We need:

- Distribution and software-use permission for each supported report variant, including Level 2 with or without valuation.
- Production template assets, ancillary documents, diagrams, terms and approved fonts; public sample reports will not be substituted for licensed assets.
- Rules for checking firm and individual entitlements, expiry and permitted users.
- Requirements for preserving wording, branding, pagination and photographic appendices, and for populating reports through a separate questionnaire and prefill interface.
- A documented acceptance/review process for the recording schema, output mappings and completed reports, including future template and standard updates.

Surveyant will distinguish source proposals from surveyor-entered answers in its capture interface. Accepted prefills are blue, human answers black; all completed report text is black. Surveyors review proposed information and retain responsibility for findings and sign-off.

## Acceptance before formal release

Pin the supplied template version and checksum centrally. Compare the complete recording schema and every output field to the authorised variant. Verify original sections, appendices, legal wording, signatures, photographs and accessibility. Test short and long answers, explicit inspection limitations, unavailable evidence, all condition ratings, non-Latin names and maximum photographic sets. Confirm that no answer is omitted, clipped, overwritten or misplaced. Review the actual rendered PDF with qualified surveyors and obtain any required supplier/RICS approval.

The currently implemented PDF route creates watermarked review copies only. Formal report issue remains blocked. The current fixed-space renderer rejects overflow and needs an approved continuation/pagination strategy for long reports before release. Its Helvetica-based answer font rejects unsupported characters explicitly; it must be replaced or extended using the centrally approved font assets before multilingual use.

References: [RICS licences](https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/home-surveys-licences), [RICS software-provider and template FAQs](https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/rics-standards-and-guidance/sector-standards/building-surveying-standards/home-surveys/home-survey-standards/home-survey-standard-frequently-asked-questions).
